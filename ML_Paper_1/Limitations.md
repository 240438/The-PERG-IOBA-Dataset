## Limitations according to author

- **Retrospective study design:** The analysis was performed on retrospectively collected clinical data, which may limit how well performance estimates translate to real-world prospective deployment.

- **Heterogeneous diagnostic categories:** Pathological cases include multiple diagnostic subtypes grouped together. This heterogeneity may reduce generalizability and can make it harder to ensure consistent performance across different diseases and patient populations.

- **Limited modalities (no retinal imaging such as OCT):** Although demographic and visual-acuity metadata were included, additional modalities—especially retinal imaging (e.g., OCT)—were not available. Incorporating imaging data could further improve diagnostic performance.

- **Need for broader clinical validation:** The author suggests that future work should combine electrophysiological signals with imaging data and evaluate models in **multi-center** and **prospective** settings to improve clinical applicability.
